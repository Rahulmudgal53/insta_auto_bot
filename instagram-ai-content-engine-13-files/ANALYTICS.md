# Analytics

## Track for each Reel when available
- Date and URL
- Pillar and topic
- Hook and format
- Video length
- Views and reach
- Watch time/retention
- Likes, comments, shares, and saves
- Profile visits and follows
- Follows per 1,000 reached
- Saves per 1,000 reached
- Shares per 1,000 reached

## Weekly review
- Top three posts and why they may have worked
- Bottom three posts and likely issues
- Best-performing pillar and hook pattern
- Retention, save, and share signals
- Follow conversion
- What to continue, stop, or test next

Use multiple posts to identify patterns; do not overreact to a single result.
