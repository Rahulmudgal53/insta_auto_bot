# Monetization Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Trust and relevance
Build consistent useful content and learn which audience problems recur.

## Phase 2 — Validate demand
Look for repeated questions, saves, shares, inbound requests, and tool/workflow interest.

## Phase 3 — Small tests
Potential options:
- Relevant affiliate partnerships
- Digital workflow templates
- Automation templates
- Practical guides
- Services or implementation support
- Sponsorships when audience fit is credible

## Guardrails
Do not let monetization weaken editorial trust. Disclose affiliate relationships where required, evaluate recommendations honestly, and never promise results that cannot be supported.
