# First 30-Day Plan

This is a validation plan, not a promise of growth. Adjust based on available time and results.

## Week 1 — Establish the baseline
- Day 1: Set up profile positioning and content tracking.
- Day 2: Demonstrate a simple AI-assisted workflow.
- Day 3: Research audience problems and score ideas.
- Day 4: Publish a tool demonstration.
- Day 5: Record a before/after productivity workflow.
- Day 6: Review production friction and refine the checklist.
- Day 7: Weekly review; record baseline metrics.

## Week 2 — Test hooks and formats
- Day 8: Publish a problem-first workflow.
- Day 9: Prepare two alternate hooks for a future Reel.
- Day 10: Publish an honest tool comparison.
- Day 11: Test a step-by-step screen recording.
- Day 12: Publish a developer/work productivity demo.
- Day 13: Review comments and questions for new ideas.
- Day 14: Weekly review; choose one format to repeat.

## Week 3 — Build repeatability
- Day 15: Publish an automation demonstration.
- Day 16: Test a concise hook and direct payoff.
- Day 17: Publish a research-to-summary workflow.
- Day 18: Create a reusable production template.
- Day 19: Publish a demo that explains a tool limitation.
- Day 20: Review saves, shares, and follows.
- Day 21: Weekly review; remove steps that slow production.

## Week 4 — Improve and validate
- Day 22: Revisit a promising topic with a new real example.
- Day 23: Publish a workflow with measurable before/after evidence.
- Day 24: Test a different CTA.
- Day 25: Publish an AI-assisted developer workflow.
- Day 26: Gather recurring audience problems.
- Day 27: Publish the strongest remaining demo idea.
- Day 28: Weekly review and compare pillars.
- Day 29: Document what worked, what failed, and why.
- Day 30: Choose next month's pillars and experiments from evidence.

## Sustainable cadence
If daily production is unrealistic, publish 3–5 high-quality Reels per week and use the other days for research, recording, editing, and review. Consistency and proof matter more than hitting an arbitrary daily quota.
