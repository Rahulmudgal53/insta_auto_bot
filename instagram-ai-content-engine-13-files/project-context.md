# Project Context — Instagram AI Content Engine

## Mission
Build a sustainable AI-assisted Instagram account focused on practical AI tools, automation, workflows, and productivity.

## Locked decisions
- Audience: global, English-first
- Format: faceless screen recordings and original visuals
- Content: original, practical, demonstration-driven
- Priority: audience growth first, with monetization considered from day one
- Style: useful and evidence-led; no motivational fluff
- Budget: zero-budget-first

## Content philosophy
Demonstrate real value. A strong Reel shows a problem, the tool or workflow, the real process, the result, and a reproducible takeaway.

Example format: “I turned a 30-minute task into a 3-minute workflow.” Only use time-saving numbers when the demonstration genuinely supports them.

## Avoid
Generic AI news, motivational quotes, recycled content, fake demos, AI-generated filler, engagement bait, and unsubstantiated claims.

## Operating workflow
Research → discover opportunities → generate and score ideas → select idea → write hook/script → perform real demonstration → record screen/create original visuals → edit → caption → human quality review → publish → analytics → weekly optimization → test monetization opportunities.

## Automation boundary
Automate or AI-assist research organization, idea generation/scoring, script drafts, hook variations, caption drafts, content calendars, checklists, analytics summaries, experiment tracking, and weekly reports.

Keep the actual tool demonstration, final claims, final quality review, and publishing approval human-controlled.

## Monetization
Potential paths include relevant affiliate partnerships, digital workflow templates, guides, and services. Validate audience trust and problem fit before pushing offers.
