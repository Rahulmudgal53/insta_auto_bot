## Initial tests
1. **Hook framing:** problem-first vs outcome-first.
2. **Demo pacing:** quick cuts vs step-by-step.
3. **CTA:** save-oriented CTA vs question CTA.
4. **Length:** concise demo vs longer tutorial.
5. **Cover:** result-focused text vs problem-focused text.

Record real outcomes. Do not invent conclusions or claim statistical certainty from tiny samples.
