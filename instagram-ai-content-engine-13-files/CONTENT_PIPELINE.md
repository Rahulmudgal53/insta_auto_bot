# Content Pipeline

1. **Research:** collect real problems, questions, and tool/workflow opportunities.
2. **Idea generation:** produce several possible demonstrations.
3. **Scoring:** evaluate problem strength, demonstrability, novelty, reproducibility, audience fit, and production effort.
4. **Selection:** choose the strongest idea that can be demonstrated honestly.
5. **Script:** hook → problem → demonstration → result → takeaway/CTA.
6. **Demonstration:** perform the actual workflow.
7. **Recording and visuals:** capture the process and create original supporting visuals.
8. **Editing:** tighten pacing, add readable captions, remove dead time.
9. **Caption:** draft a concise explanation and relevant CTA.
10. **Human QA:** verify accuracy, originality, privacy, and quality.
11. **Publish:** use supported Instagram tools; do not assume an integration is connected.
12. **Analytics:** record available performance metrics.
13. **Weekly optimization:** turn evidence into the next week's experiments.
