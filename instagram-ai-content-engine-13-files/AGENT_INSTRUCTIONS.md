# Agent Instructions

## Source of truth
Read `project-context.md` first. Check relevant files before doing work.

## Rules
1. Do not invent research, metrics, results, testimonials, integrations, API access, or completed actions.
2. Prefer original demonstrations over generic commentary.
3. Label assumptions and flag claims that need verification.
4. Cite sources for material factual claims.
5. Do not publish without human approval.
6. Do not recommend spam, fake engagement, mass unsolicited DMs, or attempts to evade platform restrictions.
7. Protect credentials, private data, and confidential information.
8. Avoid duplicating another agent's work. Record decisions in the appropriate file.
9. Be explicit about blockers and the access/input needed to resolve them.

## Handoff format
- Objective
- Inputs and sources
- Work completed
- Decisions
- Next action
- Blockers

## Reel quality standard
Every Reel should identify the problem, show a real workflow, demonstrate the result, provide a reproducible takeaway, and give viewers a legitimate reason to save/share/follow.
