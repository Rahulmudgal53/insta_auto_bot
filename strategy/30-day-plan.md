Status: DRAFT — adjust based on capacity and performance.

## Week 1 — Establish the Format
Test practical workflow demos, single-tool problem/solution videos, before/after comparisons, and one reality-check Reel.

## Week 2 — Test Hooks
Try different hook structures on comparable topics. Keep demonstrations honest and reproducible.

## Week 3 — Double Down
Prioritize topics with stronger retention, saves, shares, profile visits, and follows per reach.

## Week 4 — Systemize
Turn promising formats into repeatable series and document the production process.

## Suggested Cadence
Aim for 5–7 quality Reels per week only if production capacity allows. Consistency and quality matter more than hitting a number.

## Metrics
Track views/reach, average watch time, retention, shares, saves, comments, profile visits, follows, topic, format, and hook.
