# Monetization Roadmap

## Principle
Build audience trust first. Monetization should fit the audience's demonstrated needs and must not dictate dishonest content.

## Stage 1 — Learn
- Identify recurring audience problems from comments and analytics.
- Track tools that are genuinely useful.
- Clearly disclose affiliate relationships where applicable.

## Stage 2 — Test Lightweight Offers
- Relevant affiliate recommendations.
- Free checklist or template to learn what the audience values.
- Small digital templates or workflow guides.

## Stage 3 — Expand Based on Evidence
- Paid guides or mini-products.
- Sponsorships that fit the niche.
- A useful automation or software product if repeated demand exists.

## Guardrails
Do not promise income or results without evidence. Do not promote a tool solely because it pays. Track clicks and conversions as well as audience trust.
