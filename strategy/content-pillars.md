# Content Pillars

Status: DRAFT — validate against audience response.

## 1. AI Productivity Workflows
Demonstrate workflows that reduce repetitive work: research, email, meeting notes, documents, and personal productivity.

## 2. AI Tools Solving Specific Problems
One real problem, one tool, a demonstration, and a clear result.

## 3. Automation for Real Work
Show practical connections between tools and repeatable workflows.

## 4. AI for Developers and Creators
Useful workflows for coding, content creation, research, design, and digital work.

## 5. Before vs. After
Compare the manual process with a tested AI-assisted process.

## 6. AI Mistakes and Reality Checks
Explain limitations, failure modes, and when human verification is necessary.

## Selection Rule
Prefer ideas with a clear problem, demonstrable transformation, practical takeaway, visual proof, reproducibility, and a strong hook.
