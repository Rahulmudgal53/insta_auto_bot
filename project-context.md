# Instagram AI Content Engine — Project Context

## Mission
Build a sustainable AI-assisted Instagram account focused on practical AI tools, automation, workflows, and productivity.

## Audience and Format
- Audience: global English-speaking.
- Format: faceless Instagram Reels using screen recordings, original visuals, and clear narration or captions.
- Starting niche hypothesis: practical AI workflows, automation, and productivity for professionals and creators. Validate this through publishing and analytics.
- Budget: start with free tools and free tiers wherever practical.
- Priority: audience growth first, with monetization planned from day one.

## Core Content Philosophy
Demonstrate real value:
Problem → Tool or Workflow → Real Demonstration → Result → Reproducible Takeaway.

Example hook: “I turned a 30-minute task into a 3-minute workflow.” Use only if the result was actually tested and supported by evidence.

Avoid generic AI hype, recycled content, fake demonstrations, AI-generated filler, engagement bait, and unsupported claims. The real demonstration is the core differentiator.

## Operating Workflow
Research → Content opportunities → Ideas → Hook and script → Real demonstration → Screen recording and original visuals → Editing → Captions → Human quality review → Publishing → Analytics → Weekly optimization → Monetization experiments.

## Automation Philosophy
Automate repetitive work, not originality.

AI and automation may assist with:
- Research organization and source tracking
- Idea generation and scoring
- Script, hook, and caption drafts
- Content calendar and production checklists
- Analytics summaries and weekly reports
- Experiment and monetization tracking

Keep real demonstrations, factual verification, final creative decisions, and publishing approval human-controlled unless a workflow has been explicitly tested and approved.

## Success Metrics
Track retention and average watch time, shares, saves, profile visits, follows per reach, comments, and eventually link clicks or conversions. Follower count alone is not a sufficient success metric.

## Monetization Direction
Explore relevant affiliate recommendations, digital templates, workflow guides, sponsorships, and eventually an original software product. Protect audience trust and clearly distinguish tested results from assumptions.

## First Milestone
Publish and evaluate 10–12 quality Reels before investing in complex infrastructure. Use evidence to identify winning topics and formats.
