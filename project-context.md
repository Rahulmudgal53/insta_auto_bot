Chunk 1. Project context for Instagram AI content engine. Mission. Build a sustainable AI-assisted Instagram account focused on practical AI tools, automation, workflows, and productivity. The account targets a global English-speaking audience. Audience growth is the first priority, while monetization is built into the system from day one. Audience and format: global English-speaking audience, faceless content, screen recordings, original visuals, and real demonstrations with clear narration or captions. Primarily short-form Instagram Reels; suitable content may later be repurposed for YouTube Shorts. Core content philosophy: demonstrate real value. Preferred structure: problem, tool or workflow, real demonstration, result, reproducible takeaway. Example: I turned a 30-minute task into a 3-minute workflow. Do not build the account around generic AI news, motivational quotes, recycled content, fake demonstrations, AI-generated filler, engagement bait, or unsubstantiated claims. The actual demonstration is the core differentiator. Operating workflow: research, find content opportunities, generate and evaluate ideas, select the strongest idea, write hook and script, perform the real tool demonstration, record screen and create original visuals, edit the reel, generate captions and supporting copy, do human quality review, publish, collect analytics, analyze performance, run weekly optimization, test monetization opportunities. Automation philosophy: automate repetitive work, not originality. Automate or AI-assist research organization, content opportunity discovery, idea generation, idea scoring, script drafts, hook variations, caption drafts, content calendar, production checklists, analytics summaries, weekly performance reports, experiment tracking, and monetization tracking. Do not automate tool demonstrations or final quality review. Those must stay human. Monetization will follow once consistent value and audience trust are established. For now, focus on the content pillars and a 30-day plan before thinking about revenue. That's the unglamorous part, but it's what separates durable accounts from short-lived ones.
